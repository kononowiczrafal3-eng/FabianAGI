Privacy

Third-party providers

Fabian uses external AI providers (e.g. Groq) to generate responses. The messages of the current conversation are sent to that provider in order to obtain a reply. The provider may process this data under its own privacy policy.

Stored locally

Your conversations are stored in this browser's memory (localStorage). Only the messages needed for the current request are sent to the AI provider.

Server logs

For conversations with Fabian we store on the server: your IP address, date and time, the selected model and persona, the number of messages in the conversation, and the first 300 characters of the last message sent to the AI. We do not store full conversation contents or model responses.

Purpose: service security, abuse prevention and technical diagnostics. Legal basis: legitimate interest of the controller (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Logs are automatically deleted after 30 days and are accessible only to the service operator, after password login to the admin panel.

Controller and contact

The controller of your data is the operator of the Service, a private individual acting under a pseudonym (see the Terms of Service, Contact section). For data and log matters write to: kontakt@fabianagi.dev. You have the right to object to logging based on legitimate interest and to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority (in Poland: the President of the UODO).
